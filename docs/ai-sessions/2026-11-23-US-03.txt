  ## Summary

  - Overall: the summary/payment feature follows SPEC-US-03 v3 closely: state
  machine, masking, quote format, inline gateway, focus management, plain-text
  terms and no card data. It cannot ship as is. **F01 (blocker)** makes step 3
  unreachable for any user with a valid step 1 and also breaks the US-02
  "Continuar" flow. **F02 (major)** lets a payment be approved after the terms
  checkbox has been unchecked. The remaining findings are an ADR-004 ordering
  deviation, seed/test-coverage gaps and small cleanups.
  - Implementer judgements assessed:
    - (1) Checkbox fields shown as Sí/No, including `false`: conforms to GAP-14
  ("checkbox → Sí/No"); no finding.
    - (2) Terms from the in-memory CountryContext cache: allowed by SPEC-US-03
  §4 ("countries … may be reused from a session cache"); only plans must be
  re-fetched, and they are (`rxResource` per component instance). No finding.
    - (3) "Pagar" visible but disabled during gateway/processing: consistent
  with §5 and AC-03; no finding.
    - (4) Store clear after navigation: contradicts ADR-004:54, logged as F03.
  - Areas checked with no findings:
    - **Security:** no `innerHTML`; terms are interpolated with `white-space:
  pre-line`; `StrapiClient` is GET-only with no auth header; no PII in logs
  (only copy keys and the pricing warning); confirmation and acceptance stay in
  memory; no card keys.
    - **Non-negotiables:** single build, no per-country branches in production
  code, all copy keys exist in the CO/CL seed, standalone/OnPush/signals/control
  flow, no NgModules, fictitious test data (`1111111111`, `11.111.111-1`,
  `example.test`).
    - **Robustness:** 10 s timeout inherited; loading/error/retry states; timer
  cancelled on destroy (GAP-23).
  - Process: the branch `feat/US-03-summary-payment` matches the gate rule, the
  spec is Approved v3, and ADR-001..004 are Accepted. The commit subjects follow
  Conventional Commits with the `US-03` scope. `Refs:` trailers could not be
