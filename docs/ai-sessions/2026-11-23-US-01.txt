  ## Summary

  - **Overall:** The implementation is solid and closely follows SPEC-US-01 v2.
  Every field, label, option, rule and message comes from Strapi. The mapper and
  validators are pure functions with good tests, and the plain-text rendering
  and GET-only/no-token rules are respected. One real defect matters: Reintentar
  after a failed country lookup does not activate the country's store slot.
  That can restore and overwrite another country's data (F01). The other
  findings are small accessibility and robustness gaps, two declared deviations
  that need a human decision (F06, F08), and the P1 keyboard test that was
  deferred (F05).
  - **Areas checked with no findings:**
    - Security: no `innerHTML`, no token, no write requests, and no PII in
  `console.warn` messages.
    - Non-negotiables: one build, no hard-coded field or country content, no
  NgModules (standalone, OnPush, signals, `@if`/`@for`/`@switch`), fictitious
  data in tests (`1111111111`, `11.111.111-1`, `example.test` email).
    - ADR-004: `sessionStorage` only via `QuoteStore`; writes only on a valid
  Continuar.

  that knows the raw Strapi shapes.
